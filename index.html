How it works

Three quiet steps.

I

A private conversation

A short, confidential call. You tell me what takes up your time and what you're curious or uneasy about. There's no pitch and no obligation.

II

We meet where you are

At your home, your office or over video, on your schedule. We work on your own phone and computer with your real email and calendar, not a slideshow.

III

It stays set up

You leave with tools that work in your voice, plain written notes for later, and a direct line to me when something new comes up.

What's included

Four ways to work together.

Every engagement is one-on-one or private to your family or team. We cover coaching and setup only. Private Line does not give legal, financial or investment advice.

Private Briefing

90 minutes · your home, office or video

  • A calm, plain-English introduction to what AI can and can't do for you
  • AI set up on your phone and email, and used together on your real work
  • Privacy basics: the settings that keep what you type from being used to train anyone's model
  • A one-page written summary to keep

Done-for-you Setup

Half day · in person · follow-up visit

  • Email that drafts replies in your voice, for you to approve
  • Calendar and travel help, and voice notes turned into to-do lists
  • A personal AI assistant configured around how you actually work
  • A follow-up visit two weeks later to make sure it stuck

Concierge

Monthly · ongoing

  • Two private sessions each month
  • A direct text line during business hours
  • New tools reviewed, and set up for you only when they're worth it
  • A trusted second opinion before you or your staff adopt something new

Family & Staff Workshop

Half day · on site · up to 10 people

  • A private session for a family, a family office or household staff
  • Everyone learns the same safe habits and approved tools
  • House rules for what should and shouldn't go into AI
  • Written takeaways for everyone in the room
